Privacy at Wayfare

Wayfare is a frontend-only journey editor. There is no Wayfare account, application database, or application backend receiving your journey.

Information stored on your device

Journey names, places, descriptions, GPX coordinates, editing history, custom maps, appearance settings, resized photographs, and privacy choices are stored in your browser. Clearing this site’s browser data removes that local information.

Map, search, and routing providers

The browser requests map tiles directly from the selected map provider. Place searches go to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Route coordinates go to OSRM or, when selected, Mapbox. These services receive normal technical request data such as the IP address. A user-entered Mapbox token remains in session storage and is not part of a shared journey.

Sharing

Base64 codes and animated QR frames are produced and reconstructed in the browser. They contain the complete shared journey, including photographs. Share them only with people you trust.

Analytics and advertising

Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google AdSense are configured by the site owner and active on the deployed site. They cannot be disabled inside Wayfare. Use the vendor links below for their privacy information and controls.
